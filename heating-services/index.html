Heating Done Right, From Quote To Walkthrough

When the temperature drops in November and you flip the thermostat for the first time, the last thing you want is a furnace that won't fire. IKAD Mechanical has spent more than fifteen years quietly fixing that problem for Oakville homeowners, and quietly preventing it for the ones who hire us before something breaks.

We install, replace and maintain every part of the heating side of your home: high-efficiency natural gas furnaces, boilers for radiant systems, tank and tankless water heaters, hydronic in-floor heating for additions and full homes, and outdoor snow melting systems for driveways and walkways. One company, one warranty contact, one team who knows what's connected to what.

Furnace Installation & Replacement

Most of the furnaces we replace in Halton are between fifteen and twenty-five years old, past their warranty, running on a single stage, and burning about 30% more gas than they need to. Replacing one is straightforward, but doing it well isn't. We size every system with a Manual J load calculation rather than copying the nameplate of the unit we're pulling out, so the new furnace runs longer, quieter cycles instead of short-cycling itself into early failure.

  • 95% – 98% AFUE high-efficiency natural gas furnaces
  • Single-stage, two-stage and variable-speed modulating systems
  • Direct-vent and B-vent applications, condensate handling done to code
  • Smart thermostat setup (Ecobee, Google Nest)
  • Same-day no-heat diagnostic visits during winter

Boiler Repair & Installation

Hot-water boilers serve a smaller share of Halton homes (mostly older Old Oakville and Downtown Burlington heritage builds with cast-iron radiators), but when they need work, fewer contractors are TSSA-certified to handle them. We do boiler installation, boiler repair, boiler-to-combi conversions, hydronic loop repair, and replacement of zone valves and circulator pumps. Common brands we service include Viessmann, NTI, Lochinvar, Weil-McLain, IBC and Navien combi units.

Smart Thermostat & Humidifier Installation

Smart thermostat installation (Ecobee Premium, Google Nest) and whole-home humidifier installation (Aprilaire 700 and 800) usually pair with a furnace replacement, but we also do both as standalone upgrades. A modulating-furnace-specific thermostat (York Hx communicating control) is required to get full performance out of variable-speed furnaces, and a missing or non-working humidifier is one of the most common comfort complaints we hear about in dry Halton winters.

Heating Repair & Maintenance

About 80% of the no-heat calls we run on in January come from systems that hadn't been serviced in three or more years. A proper annual tune-up, clean burners, check gas pressure, verify draft, inspect heat exchanger, swap the filter, catches the small problems before they become a freezing house at 2 a.m. See our furnace maintenance and tune-up guide for the full checklist.

  • Pre-season furnace tune-ups
  • Same-day diagnostic for no-heat emergencies
  • Heat exchanger inspections (CO safety check)
  • Ignitor, blower motor and inducer replacements
  • Annual maintenance plans for residential customers

Frequently Asked

Halton Furnace & Heating Questions

How much does a new furnace cost in Oakville in 2026?

Most Halton furnace replacements land between $3,800 and $6,500 installed. Single-stage 95% is the budget end; variable-speed modulating 98% is the top end. The wide range comes from efficiency tier, blower type, and whether your gas line, venting, condensate drain or thermostat needs updating. Full pricing breakdown here, or see Oakville-specific notes.

What size furnace do I actually need?

Most Halton homes are 1,500–2,800 sq.ft. and need between 60,000 and 90,000 BTU, but the only way to know your specific home is a Manual J load calculation. We run one on every install. Sized correctly, your furnace runs longer cycles, quieter, and lives 5+ years longer than an oversized unit. Pair sizing with properly designed ductwork for the best comfort.

How fast can you replace a furnace that's broken down?

If we have a quote on file, we can usually be on-site within 24 hours and have the new unit running the same or next day. For total no-heat breakdowns in winter we keep a small fleet of loaner furnaces and prioritize emergency calls. See our furnace no-start troubleshooting while you wait.

Do you handle the rebate paperwork?

Yes, the Home Renovation Savings Program (which replaced Enbridge HER+ in January 2025) and the Canada Greener Homes Loan are both routine for us. We file the paperwork and your install qualifies for both if you bundle correctly. See our 2026 Ontario rebate guide.

What brands of furnace do you install?

York, Luxaire and Coleman (all part of the Johnson Controls family, so parts and service are consistent across the tiers). We don't push one brand, we recommend the right model for your home, budget and rebate fit. See our heat pump vs furnace guide if you're weighing alternatives.
