Why Our Service Area Looks This Way

IKAD Mechanical is based on Upper Middle Rd East in Oakville. Our service area expanded organically over 15 years, Halton first, then Mississauga and Hamilton through commercial work, then Brampton through industrial fitouts. Today we handle residential, commercial and custom-home HVAC across seven cities, all from a single shop, with no subcontracted installs.
