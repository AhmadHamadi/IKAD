How It Started

From One Truck In 2010 To A Halton-Wide HVAC Operation

IKAD Mechanical opened its doors in Oakville in 2010, when Ahmad bought his first service truck and started taking calls from homes in the Glen Abbey and Bronte neighbourhoods. The decision to start the company came out of a frustration that's familiar to anyone who has hired a tradesperson in the GTA: too many contractors over-promise on the phone, send a different crew than the one who quoted, and disappear after the warranty starts.

The original idea was simple. Show up when you said you would. Size the equipment to the home, not to whatever happened to be on the truck. Stand behind the work after the cheque clears. Fifteen years and 1,200+ jobs later, that's still how we operate. The trucks are newer and the team is bigger, but every install lead still walks every job from quote through final commissioning, and the owner still answers the phone on most weekdays.

Certifications, Licensing And Insurance

Working on natural gas, propane, refrigerant lines and combustion equipment without proper licensing in Ontario isn't just risky, it's illegal. We hold every credential the work requires:

  • TSSA Gas Fitter G1 and G2 licences for all natural gas and propane work
  • TSSA Authorized Contractor status for boiler, water heater and commercial gas equipment
  • ECRA/ESA licensed for HVAC-related electrical work, high voltage and low voltage
  • HRAI member in good standing (Heating, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Institute of Canada)
  • 313A and 313D refrigeration technician certificates on staff for AC and heat pump work
  • $5,000,000 commercial general liability insurance, plus WSIB coverage on every crew member, every job
  • Manufacturer-certified installer for York, Luxaire, Coleman, Daikin, Mitsubishi, Navien, Rinnai, Viessmann and NTI

We provide certificates of insurance, license numbers and TSSA contractor IDs to property managers, builders and corporate clients on request. Just ask when you're getting the quote.

How A Job Actually Runs With Us

Most of what people remember about a contractor isn't the equipment, it's the experience around it. Here's the cadence we follow on every install, residential or commercial:

  1. First call. A real person answers, weekdays 8 to 6 and Saturdays 9 to 4. After hours, you'll get a callback within an hour for genuine emergencies.
  2. On-site assessment. Usually within 2 to 5 business days for non-emergency work. We measure the home, photograph the mechanical space, run a quick Manual J load calc, and walk through what we're seeing.
  3. Written quote. Emailed the same day or next. Itemized equipment, labour, gas, electrical and venting work. Fixed price, valid 30 days.
  4. Deposit and order. Small deposit (typically 10 to 20%) confirms the install date and lets us order equipment.
  5. Install. Same lead on every visit. Drop sheets, shoe covers, daily clean-up. Most furnace and AC replacements complete in one day; custom-home jobs run on a schedule we share with the GC.
  6. Commissioning. Combustion analysis, refrigerant charge verification, gas pressure check, register-by-register temperature reading, thermostat configuration, and a homeowner walkthrough.
  7. Warranty registration. We register the manufacturer warranty in your name. Homeowners often forget this step; it matters when something fails in year eight.
  8. Follow-up. A check-in call about a week after the install to confirm everything's working as expected.
