Straight Answer

Do You Actually Need Your Ducts Cleaned?

Most homes need this every 7 to 10 years, not every year, whatever the call centres tell you. We would rather you booked it when it will actually do something. If you do not need it yet, we will say so on the phone and save you the $499.

Book it if…

  • You have just finished a renovation and drywall dust went everywhere
  • You can see dust drifting out of the supply registers when the fan kicks on
  • You bought the house from someone with pets, or a heavy smoker
  • It has been 7 to 10 years, or you have no idea when it was last done
  • There is evidence of rodents or insects in the ductwork
  • The ducts sat open during construction on a new build

Honestly, skip it if…

  • Your ducts were cleaned in the last few years and nothing has changed
  • You run a well-maintained high-MERV or 4-inch media filter and see no symptoms
  • Your real problem is hot and cold rooms, which is air balancing or duct sizing, not dirt
  • Your real problem is high static pressure, covered on our duct work page
  • Someone quoted you $89 and then found "mould" once they were inside

Frequently Asked

Duct Cleaning Questions, Answered Straight

How often do ducts actually need cleaning?

Honestly, less often than home services companies tell you. Healthy ducts in a home with good filtration are fine on a 7 to 10 year cycle. Get it done sooner if you have just renovated, you can see dust drifting out of the supplies, or you have inherited someone else's dust and pet hair. Annual duct cleaning is a sales program, not a maintenance requirement.

Why is your price $499 when I see ads for $89?

Because the $89 covers a handful of vents, and the real number arrives once the van is in your driveway. Our $499 is the whole home, every supply, every return, all the registers and the air handler, quoted before we arrive and unchanged after. If a company will not give you a whole-home price on the phone, that is the answer to your question.

What is included, exactly?

Truck-mounted vacuum on the trunk line, rotary brush agitation through every supply and return run, all registers removed and cleaned, the blower compartment and air handler interior, the filter slot, a look at the evaporator coil face, floor protection throughout, before-and-after photos from inside the ducts, a static pressure reading at the furnace and full cleanup. HST is extra.

How long does it take, and do I need to be home?

Two to four hours for a typical Halton home, depending on square footage and how many runs you have. Someone needs to let us in and be there at the end so we can walk you through the photos, but you do not need to hover. Most people carry on working in another room.

Will duct cleaning fix my hot and cold rooms?

Almost never, and we will tell you that before you book. Uneven temperatures are usually undersized returns, high static pressure or poor duct design, so the fix is air balancing or a ductwork retrofit rather than dirt removal. We take a static pressure reading during the cleaning so you get a real answer either way.

Do you clean dryer vents too?

Yes, and it is worth doing while we are already in the house. A clogged dryer vent is a genuine fire risk, not a comfort issue. Ask when you book and we will quote it alongside the duct cleaning.

Does the price change for a bigger home?

$499 covers a standard single-system home. If you have a second furnace or air handler, an unusually large home or a commercial system, we quote it separately and tell you the number before we book, never after.

Which cities does this offer cover?

Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Halton Hills, Mississauga, Hamilton and Brampton, out of our Oakville shop. If you are just outside that list, send the form anyway and we will tell you honestly whether we can get to you.
