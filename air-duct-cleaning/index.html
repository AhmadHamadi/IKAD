The Two Things Everyone Else Charges Extra For

A/C coil cleaning and furnace blower cleaning are the expensive parts of this job, and they are the two most companies quote separately once they are already inside your house. Both are in the $499.

A/C coil cleaning — included

The evaporator coil sits directly in the airstream above your furnace. When it loads up with dust it chokes airflow, drops cooling capacity and makes the whole system work harder. Cleaning it properly takes time, which is why it is usually an add-on.

Furnace blower cleaning — included

Every cubic foot of air in your house passes through the blower wheel. Caked blades move measurably less air and pull more amps. Pulling and cleaning the wheel is real work, and most quotes leave it out.

Straight Answer

Do You Actually Need Your Ducts Cleaned?

A proper clean lasts three to five years, so this is not something you should be booking every season no matter what the call centres tell you. If you do not need it yet, we will say so on the phone and save you the $499.

Book it if…

  • You have just finished a renovation and drywall dust went everywhere
  • You can see dust drifting out of the supply registers when the fan kicks on
  • You bought the house from someone with pets, or a heavy smoker
  • It has been three to five years, or you have no idea when it was last done
  • There is evidence of rodents or insects in the ductwork
  • The ducts sat open during construction on a new build

Honestly, skip it if…

  • Your ducts were cleaned in the last couple of years and nothing has changed
  • You run a well-maintained high-MERV or 4-inch media filter and see no symptoms
  • Your real problem is hot and cold rooms, which is air balancing or duct sizing, not dirt
  • Your real problem is high static pressure, covered on our duct work page
  • Someone quoted you $89 and then found "mould" once they were inside

How often do ducts actually need cleaning?

Honestly, less often than home services companies tell you. A proper clean is good for three to five years. Get it done sooner if you have just renovated, you can see dust drifting out of the supplies, or you have inherited someone else's dust and pet hair. Annual duct cleaning is a sales program, not a maintenance requirement.

How long does it take, and do I need to be home?

Two to three hours. Someone needs to let us in and be there at the end so we can walk you through the before-and-after video, but you do not need to hover. Most people carry on working in another room.

Is the dryer vent really included?

Yes, main floor or basement, in the $499. It is worth doing while we are already in the house because a clogged dryer vent is a genuine fire risk, not a comfort issue.

Will duct cleaning fix my hot and cold rooms?

Almost never, and we will tell you that before you book. Uneven temperatures are usually undersized returns, high static pressure or poor duct design, so the fix is air balancing or a ductwork retrofit rather than dirt removal. We would rather send you to the right service than take $499 for the wrong one.

A professional truck-mounted vacuum paired with a 250 PSI compressor. The vacuum pulls your whole duct system negative into the truck while the compressed air drives debris out of every branch, so the dust ends up in our tank rather than back in your house. Not a shop vac and a brush on a drill.
