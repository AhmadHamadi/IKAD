The 5 IKAD Promises

1. Right-sized equipment, every time

We run a Manual J load calculation on every furnace and AC install. Most Halton homes have furnaces 30-50% oversized because the previous contractor just copied the old nameplate. Right-sized equipment runs longer cycles, quieter, lasts 5+ years longer.

2. Fixed-price written quote

Every IKAD quote is itemized, equipment model number, capacity, all materials, labour, removal of old equipment, permits, and a fixed total. Not an "estimate" that grows. If we find something unexpected after we open the wall, we tell you before doing the work.

3. Owner on every install

Ahmad personally answers the phone, runs site visits and is on most install jobs. There is no commissioned salesperson layer adding 30% to your quote. The person who quotes you is the person responsible for the install.

4. Real same-day response

Same-day for no-heat / no-cool emergencies in Oakville and Burlington during business hours, often within 2 hours. We don't oversell this — see our customer reviews, multiple ones name the actual response time they got.

5. We handle the paperwork

Town of Oakville (or your city's) mechanical permit, TSSA inspection, ECRA/ESA electrical sign-off, Home Renovation Savings Program rebate filing, Canada Greener Homes Loan registration. All filed by us. See the 2026 rebate breakdown.

Our certifications

TSSA G1 (commercial gas fitting) and G2 (residential), ECRA / ESA electrical contractor license, HRAI membership, $5M general liability insurance, WSIB coverage. Documentation shareable on request. Full credentials on the About page.
