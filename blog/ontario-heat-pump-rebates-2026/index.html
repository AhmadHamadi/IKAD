2026 Ontario HVAC Rebates At A Glance

Halton homeowners doing a heat pump or furnace upgrade in 2026 can typically stack these programs:

  • Home Renovation Savings Program: up to $7,500 for an air-source heat pump, $12,000 for ground-source, $1,000 for a heat pump water heater (May 31, 2026 deadline)
  • Canada Greener Homes Loan: interest-free loan up to $40,000, 10-year repayment
  • Manufacturer rebates: York / Daikin / Mitsubishi promos, $200–$800 on specific models
  • Save on Energy thermostat rebates: $75–$100 on a smart thermostat

The rebate landscape in Ontario shifted significantly in January 2025. Enbridge HER+ ended. The new Home Renovation Savings Program (HRS) replaced it, jointly delivered by Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy, and the rules are simpler and the rebates are bigger. Most Halton homeowners who installed a heat pump in 2024 missed money they could have claimed under the new program if they'd waited.

Here's exactly what's available in 2026 and how to stack it.

Home Renovation Savings Program (HRS) — Your Main 2026 Rebate

The Home Renovation Savings Program is the dominant residential HVAC rebate in Ontario for 2026. It launched January 28, 2025 and runs through May 31, 2026 for registration. Key facts:

  • No energy audit required. The biggest single change from HER+. You don't need a pre-retrofit Energy Advisor visit (save $400–$600).
  • Participating contractor required. Your installer must be registered with the program (we are).
  • Direct deposit. Rebate is direct-deposited to your bank, no rebate card.
  • Stackable with the Greener Homes Loan. You can claim HRS and finance the entire upgrade through the interest-free loan in the same project.

HRS Eligibility For Halton Homes

  • You must own the home (rentals not eligible).
  • The home must be your primary residence in Ontario.
  • Equipment must meet the program's specific make/model list, we check this before quoting.
  • Install must be done by a participating contractor.
  • Registration must be submitted by May 31, 2026.

Canada Greener Homes Loan (Still Active)

  • Interest-free loan up to $40,000
  • 10-year repayment term, principal-only payments
  • Eligible work: heat pumps, insulation, windows/doors, solar PV, on-site renewables, heat pump water heaters
  • Still requires a registered Energy Advisor audit (different from HRS, this one's not waived)
  • Applied through Natural Resources Canada portal
  • You apply, get approved, do the work, get reimbursed (the loan amount), then make monthly payments

When You Still Need An Energy Advisor Audit

The HRS Program no longer requires the audit. But the Greener Homes Loan still does. So:

  • If you're only claiming HRS: no audit needed, skip it.
  • If you want both HRS and the Greener Homes Loan financing: get the audit. One audit unlocks both.
  • If you're claiming HRS only and want to finance: use a private HVAC finance partner, no audit required.

Common Reasons HRS Claims Get Rejected

  • Equipment not on the program's approved make/model list (always check before buying)
  • Installer not registered as a participating contractor
  • Rental property or non-primary residence
  • Project registered after May 31, 2026 deadline
  • Heat pump sized incorrectly (program audits some claims for proper Manual J sizing)

Municipal Programs Worth Asking About

  • Town of Oakville: occasional clean-energy property-tax loan programs (Local Improvement Charge). Worth asking your municipality at quote stage.
  • City of Burlington: Better Homes Burlington pilot program (limited availability)
  • Halton Region: Climate Action Plan funding for specific neighborhoods

How To Actually Claim Your Rebates (Step by Step)

  1. Get a quote from a participating contractor. We include rebate amounts in every quote.
  2. Register the project on the Home Renovation Savings Program portal (we do this for you).
  3. (Optional) Book an Energy Advisor audit if you want Greener Homes Loan financing.
  4. Apply for the Greener Homes Loan through Natural Resources Canada if applicable.
  5. Install the equipment with your participating contractor.
  6. Submit the invoice and equipment serial numbers to the HRS Program portal (we file this).
  7. Rebate direct-deposited within 4–8 weeks.

FAQ

Ontario Heat Pump Rebate Questions

What replaced Enbridge HER+ in Ontario?

The Home Renovation Savings Program (HRS), launched January 28, 2025, replaced the HER+ program. It's jointly delivered by Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy and covers electric heat pumps, gas furnaces, insulation, smart thermostats, heat pump water heaters, and more. No pre-retrofit energy audit is required, which is the biggest simplification from the old HER+ rules.

How much can I get for a heat pump under the HRS Program?

Up to $7,500 for an air-source cold-climate heat pump that replaces electric or fossil-fuel heating, $1,250 per ton for non-gas-heated homes, $500 per ton for gas-heated homes, and up to $12,000 for a ground-source heat pump. The exact amount depends on system type, capacity and whether you're replacing gas or electric resistance heat.

Is the Canada Greener Homes Grant still available?

No, the Grant program closed for new applicants in early 2024. The interest-free Loan program (up to $40,000, 10-year repayment) remains active and is still the most valuable financing program for heat pump and major retrofit work.

What's the deadline to apply for HRS?

Registration for the Home Renovation Savings Program closes May 31, 2026. You must register before the deadline; the actual install and rebate claim can happen after registration. We strongly recommend registering as soon as you're considering a heat pump or furnace upgrade.

Can IKAD handle the paperwork?

Yes, we are a participating contractor on the Home Renovation Savings Program. We register your project, file invoices in the format the program needs, and walk you through the rebate claim. We do this every week.
